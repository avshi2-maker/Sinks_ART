## 🏆 SHIPPED THIS SESSION: THE PRICING ENGINE ("from sketch to price offer" — the Queen module)

The core business tool: one calculation, sketch → true cost → offer, feeding both the customer offer and
the transparent Ales sheet. Built + LIVE (committed + pushed). Verified against Ales's real cost xlsx.

### The money model (Avshi's design, locked)
```
trueCost   = material (Trabelsi) + labor (days × rates) + overhead (days × ₪133) + consumables
baseOffer  = trueCost × (1 + 15%)         XYZ — transparent to Ales
finalOffer = baseOffer + artPremium       UVW — what the customer pays (premium = manual, per job)
premium split (default 50/50, editable): Avshi share + Ales bonus
Ales total = guaranteed day-rate labor income + his premium bonus  (the trust-builder "BOOM")
Avshi total = 15% commission + his premium share
```
Overhead ₪133/day = (rent 700 + electric 300 + vehicle 1000 + insurance 250 + accountant 300 + food 150
+ misc 500 = 3200) ÷ 24 workdays. All from Ales's real xlsx. VERIFIED: Goldman 2.70 → true cost ₪9,403
(material ₪4,613 at current Trabelsi settings + labor ₪4,790 @ 1.5 days), base ₪10,813, +₪5,000 premium
→ final ₪15,813, split gives Ales ₪7,000 (₪4,500 labor + ₪2,500 bonus), Avshi ₪3,910.
