KEY TECHNICAL FACTS LOCKED (corrected during session):
- Counters: women's 3400mm, men's 3200mm; both 4 basins × 600mm, 500mm deep, 250mm apron, wall-hung.
- Material: גרניט פורצלן (porcelain-granite) — architect's own label "כיור גרניט פורצלן". NOT Caesarstone.
  Two board colours: טרצו (light, flecked 120x120) + אריח שחור (smooth black slate 120x120).
- Build: 12mm double lamination (2×6mm).
- MOUNTING: continuous steel ledger + concealed cantilever arms + M12 chemical anchors; weight
  ~120kg women's / ~110kg men's; MUST confirm wall type (concrete/block = chemical anchor; drywall =
  steel backing between studs before closing wall). Structural engineer sign-off recommended.
- HIDDEN DRAIN (Ales's real method): porcelain cover cut from tile, floats on 4 feet (פקקים),
  flush with basin, 2-3mm perimeter gap all 4 sides → water into inner envelope (מעטפה) → bottle trap.
  Cover has lift-notches (rounded cut-out) on both short ends to finger-pull for cleaning. Fully hidden.
- SEAM ALIGNMENT: sink centreline + drain + faucet on one vertical line, ideally on a tile joint/centre;
  edges relate to horizontal courses; drain not on a seam. Water point set by plumber BEFORE tiling —
  coordinate centreline with plumber+architect early.
